The fifth objective is to provide interpretable feature-based facial attractiveness analysis. The project implements a dual-stream co-attention deep learning architecture that combines MobileNetV2-based networks to process both RGB facial images and semantic parsing maps. Using MediaPipe Face Mesh for precise landmark detection, the system segments the face into eight anatomical regions (eyes, eyebrows, nose, mouth, skin, hair) and employs occlusion-based sensitivity analysis to quantify each feature's individual contribution to overall attractiveness. The model, trained on the SCUT-FBP5500 dataset, produces both an overall attractiveness score on a ten-point scale and individual feature scores accompanied by color-coded heatmaps that visualize which facial regions contribute positively or negatively to perceived beauty. This module is separate from the HarmonyScore and is presented as an additional reference that provides actionable insights beyond geometric analysis, enabling users to understand not just their overall score but specifically which features enhance or detract from their facial attractiveness.
